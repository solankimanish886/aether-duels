How to Play

⚔️ Forge a Duel. Host a match — a room code appears. Send it to anyone, anywhere. The moment they join, the duel begins.

🎯 Enter the Arena. Your opponent pastes the code and you're both in the lobby. Ready up and play.

🎨 Three rounds. Each round, both players get the same prompt and 60 seconds. Draw simultaneously — you can see their strokes appear live in the corner.

🗳️ Mutual judgment. When time's up, both players vote on the better artwork. Votes reveal at once. Agreement crowns a winner — disagreement is a "Judge's Tie."

🏆 Best of three. First to two round wins takes the match, the XP, and any achievements unlocked along the way.

✋ Hand mode. Tap the hand icon in the toolbar to draw mid-air with your finger via the camera. Point your index finger to draw, open your palm to lift the brush. Win a match this way for an extra achievement.

🎨 Practice Round. Draw any prompt solo for sixty seconds — for warming up your brush.
